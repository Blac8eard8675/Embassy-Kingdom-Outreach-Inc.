About Us

Embassy Kingdom Outreach Inc.is a Christ-centered outreach ministry dedicated to helping individuals discover their identity, purpose, and calling through a personal relationship with Jesus Christ. At the heart of our ministry is a discipleship home designed to provide a safe, supportive, and Christ-centered environment where people can experience restoration, grow in their faith, and learn how to live out the Gospel in their everyday lives. We believe discipleship is more than simply attending church—it is learning to follow Jesus in every area of life. Through biblical teaching, prayer, mentorship, accountability, worship, practical life skills, community, and hands-on service, residents are encouraged to grow spiritually while developing the tools and character necessary to build a healthy and purposeful future. Our goal is to meet people where they are and walk alongside them toward where God is calling them to be. We believe every person has God-given value, purpose, and potential. Our ministry seeks to create an atmosphere where individuals can encounter the love of Christ, rebuild their lives, develop healthy relationships, discover their gifts, and become equipped to serve others. Embassy Kingdom Outreach Inc. is committed to being an embassy of God's Kingdom—a place where Christ is honored, people are welcomed, lives are restored, and disciples are equipped to make disciples. We desire to see transformed individuals become strong families, faithful servants, responsible members of their communities, and committed followers of Jesus Christ. Our vision is not simply to provide a place to stay, but to provide a place to grow, heal, learn, serve, and be sent. We believe that when people are rooted in Christ and surrounded by godly community, they can discover a new direction and walk confidently into the purpose God has placed on their lives. Embassy Kingdom Outreach Inc. exists to make disciples, restore lives, build up the body of Christ, and send people into their communities equipped to represent Jesus wherever God leads them.
